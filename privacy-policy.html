PrivacyPolicy

How BKDziti LLC collects, uses, and protects your information.

Last updated: June 24, 2026

Who We Are

BKDziti LLC (“BKDziti,” “we,” “us,” or “our”) operates the website at bkdziti.com and provides consulting, branding, media production, and web development services to businesses and individuals across industries, with specialty depth in culinary consulting and pop-up coordination. Our primary contact is AlexZornes@BKDziti.com.

Information We Collect

We collect information you provide directly to us:

  • Contact form submissions — name, email address, phone number (optional), and your message.
  • Purchase and payment data — when you purchase services through our store, payment is processed by Stripe. We do not store your full card number, CVV, or billing address. Stripe provides us with a transaction record and limited billing details.
  • Email correspondence — if you contact us by email, we retain those communications to handle your inquiry.

We may also collect limited technical data automatically:

  • IP address and general location (country/region), collected by Cloudflare for security and performance.
  • Browser type, referring page, and pages visited, collected via analytics tools if present.
  • Cookies set by third-party services (see below).

How We Use Your Information

  • To respond to inquiries and fulfill service engagements.
  • To process payments and send order confirmations.
  • To communicate project updates and deliverables.
  • To improve our website and understand how visitors use it.
  • To comply with legal obligations.

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third parties for their marketing purposes.

Third-Party Services

BKDziti uses the following third-party services, each with their own privacy practices:

  • Stripe — payment processing. Stripe Privacy Policy.
  • Cloudflare — CDN, DNS, and security. Cloudflare Privacy Policy.
  • Resend — transactional email delivery for contact form submissions. Resend Privacy Policy.
  • Google Fonts — font delivery. Google may log your IP when fonts are loaded. Google Privacy Policy.
  • Google Analytics — if active on this site, Google Analytics collects anonymized usage data. You can opt out via the Google Analytics Opt-out Add-on.

Cookies

This site may use cookies placed by Cloudflare (for security), Stripe (for checkout sessions), and Google Analytics (for usage tracking). We do not use advertising or tracking cookies of our own. You can disable cookies in your browser settings; doing so may affect checkout functionality.

Data Retention

Contact form submissions and email correspondence are retained for up to 3 years to support ongoing business relationships and legal record-keeping. Payment records are retained as required by Stripe and applicable tax law. You may request deletion of your data at any time (see below).

Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you.
  • Request correction of inaccurate data.
  • Request deletion of your data (subject to legal retention obligations).
  • Opt out of analytics tracking (see Google Analytics opt-out above).

Florida residents have rights under Florida law. California residents have rights under the CCPA. To exercise any of these rights, email us at AlexZornes@BKDziti.com.

Children’s Privacy

This site is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from minors. If you believe a minor has provided us information, please contact us and we will delete it promptly.

Security

We take reasonable steps to protect your information, including HTTPS/SSL encryption via Cloudflare and secure payment processing via Stripe. No method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure. We cannot guarantee absolute security but we implement industry-standard protections.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Material changes will be reflected in the “Last updated” date above. Continued use of the site after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
