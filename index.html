About

BKDziti

My background is in food — service, execution, atmosphere — and I’ve spent years building parallel skills in media, design, business strategy, and digital systems. The same principles that make a restaurant unforgettable apply to any business: sensory context, pacing, and compositional thinking. I use those techniques, rarely combined outside the culinary world, to engineer experiences and operations that people actually remember, whatever the industry.
