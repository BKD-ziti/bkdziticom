How to use this list

  • 1. Start with Priority = High rows on the Lead List tab (Real Estate + Restaurants) -- highest odds of same-day cash.
  • 2. Call or walk in, don't wait for a reply by text/DM alone -- speed of response is the whole pitch.
  • 3. Lead with: same-day turnaround + flat cash/Venmo price, no invoice, no contract, no subscription.
  • 4. Confirm exact address/phone via Google Maps or Facebook before heading out -- some entries list city only.
  • 5. Book back-to-back locations using your car -- cluster by neighborhood (Cape Coral Pkwy corridor, SE 47th, Del Prado Blvd) to minimize drive time.
  • 6. For real estate specifically: search Zillow/Redfin filtered to 'new listing, Cape Coral' right before you start calling -- that surfaces the exact agents with something to shoot TODAY.
